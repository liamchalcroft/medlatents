# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
experience for everyone who participates in the `medlatents` community,
regardless of background or identity.

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1. The full canonical text is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and collaborative.
- Welcome newcomers and differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Conduct that is disrespectful, exclusionary, or that makes others feel unwelcome
is not tolerated. This includes personal attacks, publishing others' private
information without permission, and any conduct that would reasonably be
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting and Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the
project maintainer at **liamchalcroft@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed
and handled confidentially. Maintainers may take any action they deem
appropriate, up to and including a temporary or permanent ban from the project's
spaces, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant v2.1.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and in public spaces when
an individual is representing the project or its community.
